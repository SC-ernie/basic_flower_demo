ALOS FLORAL DEMO – PRICING + PITCH NOTES

Suggested price ranges for a small-business site like this:

1) Starter demo / simple one-page site:
- $250 to $400
- Good if you are mainly building a clean online presence and linking back to Instagram.

2) Better custom package:
- $450 to $700
- Includes custom design, mobile responsiveness, copy cleanup, Instagram integration, basic edits, and deployment help.

3) If they want more:
- +$50 to $100 for domain / hosting setup help
- +$25 to $75 per month for maintenance, text changes, photo swaps, holiday promos, or seasonal updates

Good beginner offer:
- Charge around $350 to $500 if you are making the full site, editing copy, packaging the files, and helping publish it.

Simple pitch angle:
"I made a quick demo site based on your Instagram so you can see how your bouquets could look on a real website. It keeps Instagram as the main place to order, but gives you a more professional online presence and an easier way for customers to browse your work. If you like it, I can customize the text, photos, and order flow and get it live for you."

What to emphasize in the pitch:
- More professional than only using Instagram
- Easier for new customers to trust the business
- Easier to share one website link
- Still keeps their current DM ordering workflow
- Can highlight occasions, pricing ranges, and delivery / pickup info

Short DM template:
"Hey! I put together a small website demo for Alos Floral Arrangements based on your Instagram page. It matches your style and sends customers back to your Instagram DMs to order. I thought it could help you look even more established online. If you want, I can show you the demo and we can talk about getting something like this live for your business."
